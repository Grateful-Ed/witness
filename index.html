Step 1 — Speak the load

Hold the button and describe the pallet in front of you: how many boxes, how the stack sits, the wrap, anything damaged, anything that needs doing. Say it the way you'd say it to the next person on the dock. Release the button and your words become the receiving record.

Test loads

Two loads to practice on. Pick one, look it over from all four sides, then hold the button below and say what you see — before anyone tells you what's wrong with it. Picking a load only labels your scan; you can also skip both and scan any real pallet.
