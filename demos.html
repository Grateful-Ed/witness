Witness — live demos

Every demo, one link each

Witness is a warehouse run by voice and vision: the worker talks to Meta AI and the WMS updates itself. Each demo below opens on its own — pick one and just start. Nothing here can break the real record; the practice copy and the kitchen reset themselves.

Receiving cross-check

Module one: every load gets two independent reads — a spoken scan and a photo scan — compared into an OS&D report.

Start here if you have two minutes: speak a load, photograph it, watch the flags.

Open the cross-check

Walk the Shift

The game: work a real shift on the warehouse map — inbound dock worker, outbound loader, replenishment, cycle counter — by talking to META.

Opens a practice copy of the warehouse on the Receiving view. Pick a shift type and say "Complete task."

Walk the shift

WMS console

The whole warehouse, live: the map, receiving, putaway, orders, labor — and the Ontology page, where the building's dynamic digest and the slang it has learned live.

Look around freely. Ask the building a question from the Witness page.

Open the console

Warehouse glasses

The receiving dock through the wearer's eyes: you speak what you see on the pallet, and META checks it against the camera.

Hold anywhere to talk. Double-tap to capture a photo.

Open the warehouse glasses

Kitchen glasses

The home-scale demo: scan a grocery receipt and META reads it, creates the putaway job, and walks you item by item to its spot.

Double-tap to scan any grocery receipt — not just ours. Then hold to talk and say "Start Groceries putaway."

Open the kitchen glasses

The desk record

The warehouse's own record — bills of lading, staged freight, flags, and the full event history the glasses and the console write to.

Open it beside the glasses: scan on the glasses, watch the record move here.

Open the desk record
